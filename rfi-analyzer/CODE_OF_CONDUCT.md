# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help: being welcoming and respectful, accepting constructive
feedback gracefully, focusing on what's best for the project, and showing
empathy toward other contributors.

Things that don't: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and any
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior. They may
remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with this Code of
Conduct.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior by opening a
private report through GitHub, or by contacting a maintainer directly. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will remain confidential.
